## 📖 Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Take control of your health journey with **FoodieBite**, your premium, dark-themed personal nutrition assistant. Optimized for mobile-first convenience, FoodieBite dynamically ranks food recommendations matching your specific dietary choices, local weather, and health goals!

### 🌟 Key Features:

#### 1. Smart Seasonal Food Recommendations
Our local food engine recommends fresh, light salads during warm summers and hot, protein-dense soups during cold winters. Get perfect food picks dynamically adjusted to your regional weather!

#### 2. Personalised Dietary Profiles
Whether you are Vegetarian, Vegan, Eggetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Keto, or looking for Diabetic Friendly picks, FoodieBite instantly filters out allergens and unwanted categories to display exactly what suits you.

#### 3. Secure Medical Report Locker
Upload your blood panels or annual medical reports securely. All documents are stored in highly secure, private cloud lockers (powered by Cloudinary), making it easy to reference your health history anytime.

#### 4. Instant Food Search & Barcode Scan
Search through the global Open Food Facts database in seconds or scan a barcode to instantly retrieve comprehensive macro breakdowns (calories, protein, carbs, fats) and nutrition grades (A to E).

#### 5. Premium Mobile-First Dark Theme
Designed with premium typography (Inter), harmony gradients, and fluid micro-animations for an outstanding, sleek, battery-saving dark-mode experience.

### 🔒 Privacy & Safety:
We take your privacy seriously. All data transfers are encrypted in transit, and Cloud Firestore security rules ensure that **only you** can access or modify your personal dietary profile and documents. No third-party data sharing, ever.
